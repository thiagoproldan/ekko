### <a id="1023"></a>1023. PLAN proposed 2026-10-01, for the user's decision. It rests on findings note 1022 and on the research.

PRINCIPLE: the prime cannot grow with the board, and should not.

- Claude Code keeps at most 10,000 characters of a hook's output (note [165](../notes.md#165)).
- Anthropic's context engineering treats context as a finite budget: the smallest set of high-signal tokens up front, the rest reached just in time with tools.
- So the plan changes what the fixed part picks, and strengthens the just-in-time channels.
1. Fix 1020 first: rank the slots by creation, which needs no new field, with a test that enumerates every kind of update. It removes the distortion, but recovers nothing: the procedures fetched, 234 and 574, are old.
2. Measure before changing the prime: three replay arms on both periods, offline.
   - (a) Focus: the lasting notes the work in focus cites or holds, meaning the tasks in progress and ready, and the handoff.
   - (b) In-degree: the lasting notes most cited by other items, as 'procedure 234' is (note [340](337.md#340), item 7).
   - (c) Creation order.

   Set the bar before reading the numbers: an arm earns the slots if it lifts the recall of fetched gotchas, procedures and decisions to half or more, with the same 5 lines.

3. Procedures hold steps:
   - 234 goes back to its steps, about 2,400 of its 8,714 characters (-73%).
   - Each release's record goes to its release task, which already carries a DONE note.
   - Its lessons go into steps or gotchas.

   ACE (arXiv 2510.04618) keeps playbooks as itemized entries changed by local updates, and saw a rewritten context collapse from 18,282 tokens to 122. On this board, note 184 grew to 9,125 characters and misled a session (note [340](337.md#340)).

   Tokens are not the argument: the dated entries are about 1,600 tokens a fetch (an estimate at 4 characters a token). At r(2 + 0.1T), with T between 50 and 300, that is 11k to 51k units a fetch, under 0.3% of the bill over 11 fetches in 17 days. The gain is signal, and the slot 234 takes at every release.

4. Cues: give a literal cue to each gotcha about a command, and replay the last 30 days of Bash commands to count catches against false alarms (note [402](337.md#402)'s replay). Then offer the cues for the user to turn on. Only 832 has one today.
5. Private at birth. The math, from what was measured:
   - p = 154/926 = 16.6% of visible items carry private content, 20.2% with the notes on their tasks;
   - at about 46 notes a day, that is about 8 a day (an estimate);
   - the scan, built from the categories already known, missed 16 of 153 (10.5%), all in categories it did not know;
   - only the writer knows, as it writes, that a new name belongs to a client or a colleague.

   Expected private items that reach a docs commit = n \* p \* m_writer \* m_scan, and the user's read of the diff is the last layer. m_writer is not measured; one note in this session (1012) was missed by its own writer.
