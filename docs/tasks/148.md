## Notes

### <a id="149"></a>149. Measured 2026-09-14: with phases early then late, a task in early blocked by a task in late is accepted without a word…

Measured 2026-09-14: with phases early then late, a task in early blocked by a task in late is accepted without a word, and the roadmap shows nothing wrong. Phases are a total order, dependencies a partial order, and nothing requires the phase of a blocker to come no later than the phase of what it blocks. Relevant to #137, the roadmap by phase: a roadmap whose phases contradict its dependencies cannot be walked in order.
