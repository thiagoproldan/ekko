Lock waiters poll every 50 ms and starve under contention: wait in the kernel's queue, a blocking flock on a thread with the 5 s timeout kept

Four writers in a closed loop on a copy of this board (target/evals/analise-final/scripts/contention.py): p50 8 ms, but p99 318 ms and max 1.57 s; strace shows 212 EAGAIN in 240 writes and fsync at most 19 ms, so the disk is not it: a sleeping waiter misses the sub-millisecond window between one write and the next. Exponential backoff from 1 ms did not help (p99 558 ms). A blocking flock on a helper thread that hands the descriptor back, the timeout on the channel: 2 writers at most 18 to 22 ms (514 before), 4 writers at most 43 ms, latency near k times the hold time as fair queueing predicts; every lock and concurrency test passes (in the patch). In real use contention is rare, about (k-1) x rate x hold = 0.1% of writes at one write per 30 s per agent, so the gain is bulk scripts, and the 5 s timeout staying out of reach.
